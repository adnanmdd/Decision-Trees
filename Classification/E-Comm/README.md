 <h2>E-Commerce Customer Prediction</h2> 
 
 <h3>Problem Overview</h3> 
 <p> This project predicts whether an e-commerce customer is likely to make a purchase using a <strong>Decision Tree Classifier</strong>. Customer data such as age, browsing time, pages visited, and previous purchases are analyzed to identify purchasing patterns. The model is trained using historical data and then used to predict the behavior of new customers. </p>

<h3>Future Benefits</h3> 
<ul> <li>Improve targeted marketing and personalized offers.</li> <li>Increase sales and customer conversion rates.</li> <li>Reduce unnecessary marketing costs.</li> <li>Provide better product recommendations.</li> </ul>
